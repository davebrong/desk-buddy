Reading the glass

  • Black is invisible. In the prism only lit pixels show, so dark eyes on the blob or the orb become see-through holes.
  • Thin lines fade. From your chair the screen is about the size of a postage stamp. Bold shapes carry; 1-pixel detail mostly won't.
  • Drawn live vs. sprite frames. Characters drawn in code animate smoothly for free. Shaded 3D looks become stored frames, about 115 KB each at full size, so each new pose costs memory.
