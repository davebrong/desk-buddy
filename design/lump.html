What makes it feel hand-made

  • Separate drawings. Each pose is its own lopsided silhouette, so he slumps, hunches and stretches instead of scaling.
  • Line boil. Every drawing has three slightly different tracings that swap every two frames, the shimmer you get from redrawing by hand.
  • Holds and snaps. Poses are held for different lengths, so the timing has accents instead of an even, computer-smooth flow.
  • Off-register fill. The colour sits a few pixels off the line, like a print that slipped.
  • Glowing line. Black ink disappears in the prism, so on the glass his outline is drawn in warm cream instead.
