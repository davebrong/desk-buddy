Honest read: at menu bar size Lump is a tiny orange blob with two dots. Mood comes through in his colour, his eyes and the way he moves, not in the props. The laptop and envelope turn to mush. The peek reads best, and the popover (next) is where he gets to be full size. One snag: in "Needs you" he squashes down between bounces, so in the peek he briefly ducks out of sight. The bar would want its own needs-you drawing that stays up.

4 · On the desktop

A little window that sits on the Dock

A see-through, always-on-top window about 90 pt tall. He sits on the Dock's edge; click him and he waves. You can drag him anywhere.

Variant: hidden until he's needed, then he peeks up from the bottom corner. Try "Needs you".
On a light wallpaper he switches to dark ink, like the paper version.
Which Lump is in charge

Handing off between the desk and the laptop

The bridge already knows enough to decide this on its own:

  • At the desk (the monitor Lump hangs off is plugged in and his heartbeat is fresh): the desk Lump nudges; the Mac one stays a quiet peek in the menu bar, no sounds.
  • Away (laptop only, or desk Lump offline): the Mac Lump takes over: notch/peek alerts and the ocarina sounds.
  • Either way, both show the same mood, since they're fed by the same bridge.
